Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's exactly what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: April 2026
01

Information We Collect

We collect your name, email address, and reservation details when you register and use Studify. We do not collect payment card information — all payment processing is handled through mock flows for demonstration purposes.

02

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to manage your account, process reservations, send confirmation emails, and improve our platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

03

Data Security

Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing. JWT tokens are used for secure session authentication. All data is stored in a secure cloud database (Supabase) with encrypted connections.

04

Studify stores your session token in browser localStorage to keep you logged in. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

05

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us. We will process your request within 7 business days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a reservation?

Browse available spaces, click Reserve, fill in your date, time, duration, and number of persons, then confirm. You'll receive an email when the owner approves.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the space owner — usually within a few hours. Premium members get auto-confirmation instantly with no waiting.

What is Premium membership?

Premium (₱99/month) gives you auto-confirmed reservations, 10% discounts on paid spaces, and WiFi speed badges for all space cards.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes. Go to My Reservations in your dashboard and click Cancel. The seat count will be restored automatically.

How do I become a space owner?

Contact us via email or phone. Our admin team will verify your space and set up an owner account linked to your location.

Is my payment information safe?

Studify uses a mock payment system for demonstration purposes. No real card data is stored or processed.

Feature

⚡ Real-Time Availability

Know exactly how many seats are available at any space before you even leave the house.

How It Works

1
Space owners check in students — when a student arrives, the owner taps Check In and the seat count drops in real time.
2
Socket.io pushes updates — every browser viewing Studify gets the updated seat count instantly, no refresh needed.
3
Status badges update — each space card shows 🟢 Open, 🟡 Busy, or 🔴 Full automatically based on the seat count.
🟢
Open
Seats available
🟡
Busy
3 or fewer seats left
🔴
Full
No seats available

The space's average rating recalculates instantly and updates on the space card for all users to see.

Each user can rate a space once. You can update your rating anytime.
